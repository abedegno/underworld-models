# The original sprites

The pictures in this folder are the creature sprites and object icons of Ultima Underworld (1992). They are the game's artwork and belong to the owners of Ultima Underworld. They are not covered by this repo's licence.

They are shown here only so that people can compare the fan-made models with the original. `tools/sprites.py` draws them from your own copy of the game; the repo holds no game files.
